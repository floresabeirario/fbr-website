Some gifts end up in a drawer. Some stay in the memory. And some stay on the wall for decades.

If you are thinking about what to give a bride, whether as a family member, close friend, or colleague, this guide gets straight to the point.

## The problem with conventional wedding gifts

The wedding list solves the practical problem: the home gets equipped, no duplicates, everyone is satisfied.

But if you want something outside the list, something personal, something with memory, something the bride will not forget, the choice becomes difficult.

Experiences? Good, but fleeting.
Jewellery? Depends a lot on taste.
A trip? Often already planned.

What rarely appears on lists, and carries enormous emotional value, is the **preservation of the wedding bouquet**.

## What it is and why it works as a gift

Bouquet preservation transforms the wedding flowers into a framed piece of botanical art. The bouquet that would have wilted within days stays on the wall of the home for decades.

It is a gift with these characteristics:

- **Completely personalised:** it is made with the flowers from that specific wedding, no two are the same
- **Lasts decades:** with museum anti-UV glass, the frame can last 50 to 100 years
- **Visible every day:** it does not sit in a drawer, it hangs in the living room or bedroom
- **Has a story:** everyone who sees the frame asks where it came from

No other wedding gift has all of these characteristics at once.
